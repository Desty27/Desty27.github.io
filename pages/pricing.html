What's included in the free 1-month SEO for Professional? ▾
The free SEO month includes Initial Review & Analysis, On-Page Optimisation, Local Search Optimisation, and a Ranking Report — exactly what's in our ₹10,000/month SEO package, at zero cost. You get to see real results before committing to a monthly plan.
Is the domain renewal mandatory after the first year? ▾
Yes, domain names are renewed annually. For Starter it's ₹599/year (subdomain on corner-stone.me) and for Professional it's approximately ₹1,599/year for your own .com domain. These are standard industry rates with no markup from our side.
Can I switch from Starter to Professional later? ▾
Absolutely. We can migrate your Starter website to a custom domain and upgrade all features at any time. We'll only charge the difference and help make the transition seamless.
Are the digital marketing packages locked in contracts? ▾
No lock-in contracts. Every digital marketing package runs month-to-month. You can pause, switch, or cancel anytime. We earn your trust through results, not contracts.
How long does it take to build and launch the website? ▾
Starter websites are typically live within 24–48 hours of receiving your business details. Professional websites take 3–5 business days to complete setup, including domain configuration, email setup, and SEO foundations.
Can I combine packages — e.g., Professional website + SEO + Maps? ▾
Yes! In fact, most clients get better results combining a Professional website with our SEO and Maps packages. Contact us for a bundled quote — we often offer additional savings for combined packages.
